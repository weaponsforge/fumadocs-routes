## Secret Tips

### Tele-Nova

This tip combines the Wizard's **Teleport** and **Nova** skills to escape interruption and mob attacks while charging the Nova skill.

1. Cast Nova: press the keyboard shortcut to the Nova skill + long-press the right mouse button to charge.

2. Teleport: press the keyboard shortcut to the Teleport skill + left click the mouse anywhere in the screen to teleport, avoiding mobs.

   > **IMPORTANT**: Do not let go of the right mouse button (step 1)

3. Repeat left-clicking the mouse anywhere in the screen (step 2) until the Nova finishes charging and explodes.

This is a <u>bug</u>, patched in the later MU Online versions.
